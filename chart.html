Profit lock: a fast safety stop
When a trade's profit reaches the trigger, its stop loss is moved to the price that locks in your minimum profit, and if the price then reverses you are stopped out with at least that. A stop of yours that is already better (for example you dragged it to lock 1.5) is left alone; one that is worse, or missing, is raised to the lock. Amounts are in your account currency, per trade, before commission. On a live MT5 account it runs inside the Python server (checks 10 times a second, keeps working with the chart closed).

Flip SL: stop and target after the order fills
When an order fills, the stop loss moves to the extreme of the last candle of the opposite colour: for a SELL the high of the last green candle, for a BUY the low of the last red candle. Colours come from the SMMA below. Then the take profit is set from the new stop distance.
